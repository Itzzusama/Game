Chapter 04

Quranic Verses

Quranic Verses For Our Journey

Words of Allah that guide our hearts and unite our souls

وَمِنْ آيَاتِهِ أَنْ خَلَقَ لَكُم مِّنْ أَنفُسِكُمْ أَزْوَاجًا لِّتَسْكُنُوا إِلَيْهَا وَجَعَلَ بَيْنَكُم مَّوَدَّةً وَرَحْمَةً

"And of His signs is that He created for you from yourselves mates that you may find tranquility in them; and He has put between you affection and mercy."

Surah Ar-Rum 30:21

This verse beautifully describes marriage in Islam - it's not just a contract, but a sign of Allah's creation based on tranquility (Sakina), affection (Mawaddah), and mercy (Rahmah).

هُنَّ لِبَاسٌ لَّكُمْ وَأَنتُمْ لِبَاسٌ لَّهُنَّ

"They are clothing for you and you are clothing for them."

Surah Al-Baqarah 2:187

This verse uses the metaphor of clothing to show how spouses protect, cover, and care for each other - complete intimacy, comfort, and trust.

الرِّجَالُ قَوَّامُونَ عَلَى النِّسَاءِ بِمَا فَضَّلَ اللَّهُ بَعْضَهُمْ عَلَىٰ بَعْضٍ وَبِمَا أَنفَقُوا مِنْ أَمْوَالِهِمْ

"Men are caretakers of women by [right of] what Allah has given one over the other and what they spend [for maintenance] from their wealth."

Surah An-Nisa 4:34

This doesn't mean dominance, but rather responsibility and care. A husband's role is to provide, protect, and be merciful - a sacred trust from Allah.

وَاللَّاتِي يَأْتِينَ الْفَاحِشَةَ مِن نِّسَائِكُمْ فَاسْتَشْهِدُوا عَلَيْهِنَّ أَرْبَعَةً مِّنكُمْ ۖ فَإِن شَهِدُوا فَأَمْسِكُوهُنَّ فِي الْبُيُوتِ حَتَّىٰ يَتَوَفَّاهُنَّ الْمَوْتُ أَوْ يَجْعَلَ اللَّهُ لَهُنَّ سَبِيلًا

"And live with them in kindness. For if you dislike them - perhaps you dislike a thing and Allah brings forth a great deal of good through it."

Surah An-Nisa 4:19

Even when there are challenges in marriage, we're commanded to treat our spouses with kindness, as Allah may bring goodness through our patience.

وَتُؤْتُونَ أَحَدَهُنَّ قِنطَارًا فَلَا تَأْخُذُوا مِنْهُ شَيْئًا

"And give the women [upon marriage] their [bridal] gift as an obligation."

Surah An-Nisa 4:4

The Mahr (gift) is a sign of respect, honor, and commitment - a tangible expression of the groom's sincerity in the marriage.

وَالَّذِينَ آمَنُوا وَعَمِلُوا الصَّالِحَاتِ سَنُدْخِلُهُمْ جَنَّاتٍ تَجْرِي مِن تَحْتِهَا الْأَنْهَارُ

May Allah unite our hearts in His path, Ameen

Chapter 05

Hadith

Hadith On Marriage

Wisdom from the Prophet Muhammad (PBUH) about love and companionship

The Best Among You

"The best of you are those who are best to their wives, and I am the best among you to my wives."

- Sunan At-Tirmidhi

Lesson: Good character towards a spouse is not a weakness - it's the hallmark of the best believers. The Prophet (PBUH) himself was the kindest to his wives.

The Sweetest Thing

"The world is but a collection of things, and the best thing in the world is a good woman."

- Sunan Ibn Majah

Lesson: A righteous wife is the greatest blessing in this temporary life. Her virtue and character are priceless.

Gentleness in Marriage

"Be kind to women! For woman was created from a rib, and the most crooked part of a rib is the top. If you try to straighten it, you will break it; and if you leave it, it will remain crooked. So be kind to women."

- Sahih Al-Bukhari

Lesson: Women have their own nature and personality. Accepting this with kindness, rather than harshness, is the Islamic way.

Among You Like Guests

"O Prophet, tell the believing men to lower their gaze and guard their modesty... And tell the believing women to lower their gaze and guard their modesty."

- Surah An-Nur 24:30-31

Lesson: Modesty and respect are foundational. A marriage built on mutual respect and honor lasts.

The Greatest Blessing

"When a man loves his wife and she loves him, they will be in Paradise together. For love is like a tree whose roots are in the hearts of believers."

- Islamic Teaching

Lesson: Mutual love in marriage is not just an emotion - it's a path to Jannah, the reward in the Hereafter.

Fulfill Her Rights

"The best of you are those who treat their wives well. I have treated my wife well."

- Sunan Ibn Majah

Lesson: Taking care of a wife's physical, emotional, and spiritual needs is not optional - it's a command from Allah.

May Allah make us among the best to our spouses

Ameen Ya Rabb Al-Alamin

Chapter 06

Rights & Duties

Rights & Duties

Understanding our Islamic responsibilities to each other

Husband's Rights Upon His Wife

👫
Obedience in Allah
To obey him in matters that are pleasing to Allah, and to follow the rules of Islam within the marriage.
"And they [wives] have rights over the men, similar to the rights of the men over them." - Quran 2:228
🏡
Guardianship of Home
To manage the household properly and guard it in his absence - both physically and morally.
"The best women among you are those who are kind to their husbands and maintain their homes well."
🤝
Marital Intimacy
To fulfill the marital relationship in a kind and respectful manner, both physically and emotionally.
"Live with them in kindness." - Quran 4:19

Husband's Duties Towards His Wife

💰
Financial Support
To provide halal sustenance and meet all her physical needs. This is not a burden, but a sacred responsibility.
"Men are caretakers of women... because of what they spend from their wealth." - Quran 4:34
💚
Kindness & Respect
To treat her with kindness, compassion, and dignity. Never with harshness, contempt, or cruelty.
"The best of you are those who are best to their wives." - Hadith
🛡️
Protection & Leadership
To lead the family with wisdom, protect her honor and family, and guide them towards Allah.
"Each of you is a shepherd and is responsible for his flock." - Hadith

The Beautiful Balance

Aspect Wife's Role Husband's Role Mutual
Love & Affection 💕 Give love freely 💕 Cherish & protect ❤️ Unconditional
Respect 🙏 Honor his role 🙏 Honor her dignity 🤝 Equal worth
Communication 🗣️ Express needs 🗣️ Listen with care 💬 Open dialogue
Faith 🕌 Guard deen 🕌 Lead by example 📖 Walk together
Forgiveness 🌸 Forgive quickly 🌸 Ask forgiveness ✨ Release grudges

The Core of Islamic Marriage

Neither spouse is superior. Instead, we have complementary roles - each with their own strengths and responsibilities. When both fulfill their duties with love and respect, the marriage becomes a source of mercy, tranquility, and partnership in worshiping Allah.

United in Faith, Divided in Beauty

May Allah guide us to fulfill our duties with excellence - Ameen
