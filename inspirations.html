A Reflection on Our Journey

Marriage is not about finding the perfect person. It's about two imperfect people deciding that they're going to be perfect for each other. It's about choosing to see the best in one another, even when the world sees their flaws. It's about building something that's bigger than both of us.

In Islam, marriage is a sacred contract - a half of faith. This means that together, we complete something spiritually significant. Our marriage is not just about us - it's about fulfilling a commandment from Allah, raising the next generation, and being examples of mercy and kindness.

Every challenge we face together makes us stronger. Every moment of forgiveness brings us closer. Every act of kindness plants seeds for a thousand future moments of joy.

We are enough. We are blessed. We are forever.

Two hearts, one faith, infinite love. This is what we're building. This is what we're creating. And this is what will last forever.
