My Promises To You

A vow to love, honor, and cherish you in the way of Islam

1

To Lead with Kindness

I promise to be your protector and guide, following the example of Prophet Muhammad (PBUH). As the Quran says: "The best of you are those who are best to their families" - I will treat you with utmost compassion and respect.

2

To Honor Your Worth

You are my equal in dignity and worth. I promise to listen to you, value your opinions, and make decisions together. In Islam, a wife is a queen in her home, and I will ensure you feel that honor every single day.

3

To Strengthen Our Faith

I promise to walk with you towards Allah. We will pray together, learn the Quran together, and raise a family rooted in Islamic values. "O Allah, grant me love for her as you have given me love for my own self."

4

To Provide and Protect

I promise to work hard to provide for our family with halal income. Your comfort and security are my responsibility, not as a burden, but as a privilege and duty I carry with pride.

5

To Forgive and Seek Forgiveness

I promise that when we have disagreements, I will forgive quickly and ask for forgiveness sincerely. The Prophet (PBUH) said: "Does one of you beat his wife like a beat a slave, and then embrace her?"- I will always choose gentleness.

6

To Be Your Best Friend

Beyond being your husband, I want to be your closest confidant and best friend. Someone you can laugh with, cry with, and share your deepest dreams and fears with, always.

Forever Yours in Allah's Path
